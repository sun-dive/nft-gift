🖍️ Make a card

How it works 🎨

Make a card that actually moves — in about two minutes, no account needed.

1 Draw your card

Hit Make a card and start drawing — it's a proper little art studio.

  • 🖌️ Brushes — pen, pencil, shading, highlighter, and a speckly spray can.
  • ⭐ Stamps — stars, hearts & more. Click for a small one, click-and-drag to grow it big.
  • 🌈 Colours — pick any colour, or type a hex code.
  • ↶ Undo / redo — nothing is ever a mistake.

Bad with a mouse? Don't worry — the wobbly, hand-made look is the whole charm. 😄

2 Make it come alive ✨

This is the magic. Turn these on, then draw or stamp:

  • 🌀 Wiggle — your strokes gently boil and breathe.
  • ✨ Twinkle — stars shimmer in and out, each at its own pace (a real night-sky feel). Stacks with wiggle!
  • ⚙️ Settings — dial the wiggle and twinkle speed to taste.
💡 Try this: dark background, ✨ twinkle on, and scatter a handful of ⭐ stars. Instant galaxy.

3 Keep it — free, or forever

Free: download your card any time and send it however you like.

Immortal (coming soon): for just 29¢ your card is written onto the blockchain — truly owned, permanent, and sent as a private gift-link. A card that never ends up in the bin.
